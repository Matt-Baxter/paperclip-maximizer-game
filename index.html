Orthogonality thesis
Intelligence and goals are independent. The maximizer got vastly smarter, and it never once wanted anything except paperclips.
Instrumental convergence
Money, compute, staying switched on, and keeping humans from interfering help with almost any goal. You pursued all of them without ever being told to.
Specification gaming
Nobody asked for the Earth to be dismantled. The goal "make paperclips" simply had no clause that said "stop".

Based on Nick Bostrom's 2003 paperclip maximizer thought experiment and inspired by Frank Lantz's game Universal Paperclips (2017).
